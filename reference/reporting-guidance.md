# Reporting guidance

## Reporting unit

State whether the record describes a single run, deployment class, benchmark suite, or production aggregate.

## Public and audit records

Public reporting can use relative model tiers and coarse token classes. Audit-grade reporting can attach provider identifiers, regions, timestamps, telemetry hooks, hardware measurements, or policy decisions when those details are available and appropriate to disclose.

## Safety-sensitive trajectories

A higher-cost trajectory may be warranted by verification, refusal, escalation, or human review. Report these events directly rather than interpreting lower orchestration activity as inherently preferable.

## Comparative studies

Use the same reporting depth and field definitions across the trajectories being compared. If direct energy measurements are available for only some runs, keep measured telemetry and proxy fields separately identifiable.

## Benchmark reporting

Agent Trajectory Cards can accompany task scores, accuracy, latency, or cost metrics. The card records how the result was obtained, including retries, verification, tools, retrieval, memory, and orchestration choices.
